## 1. What the School authorizes

The School authorizes its teachers to use the Platform with students in their
classes, and authorizes the Platform to create student accounts via the
School's Google Workspace domain(s):

- Authorized sign-in domain(s): `⟨district.org⟩`, `⟨student.district.org⟩`

## 2. Age eligibility

The Platform's student accounts are limited to students **13 or older** in
v1. Teachers assert eligibility per class at class creation; the assertion is
recorded (timestamp, teacher, policy version). The School confirms its
teachers are authorized to make that assertion.

☐ *(Optional, later arc — not available in v1)* The School additionally
authorizes under-13 use under COPPA's school-consent provision. **Leave
unchecked; the platform will decline under-13 sign-ins regardless in v1.**

## 3. Data handling

The Platform collects and processes only the data listed in the attached
[data map](data-map.md), for the sole purpose of providing the classroom
service. No sale of data, no advertising, no behavioral tracking. The
Platform's subprocessors: Supabase (database/auth), Cloudflare (hosting).

## 4. Retention

The default retention windows in the attached
[retention policy](retention-policy.md) apply unless the School specifies
otherwise here:

- Override (optional): ⟨e.g., "delete all student data 90 days after school
  year end"⟩

## 5. School rights

The School may, by written notice: (a) request export of its students' data;
(b) request deletion (fulfilled within 30 days); (c) revoke this
authorization, which disables the domain gate for new sign-ins immediately.

## 6. Incident notice

The Platform notifies the School's contact below within 72 hours of
discovering a breach affecting its students' data.
